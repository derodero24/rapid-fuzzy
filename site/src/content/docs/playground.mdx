Everything on this page runs in your browser with the WebAssembly build of the latest published `rapid-fuzzy`. Type a query to search, or paste your own list. Turn on **Compare** to run the same query through fuse.js and fuzzysort and see their results and timings side by side.

<Playground />

Timings are the average of repeated runs in your browser's WebAssembly engine, so they are slower than the native Node.js addon in the [benchmarks](/rapid-fuzzy/benchmarks/). The libraries match differently (see [Why rapid-fuzzy?](/rapid-fuzzy/comparison/)), so their result lists will not be the same.
